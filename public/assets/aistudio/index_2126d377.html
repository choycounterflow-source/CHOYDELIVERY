Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

Last Revised: November 2026

1. Fraud-Prevention & Private Customer KYC Storage

By uploading your government-issued valid ID and selfie photograph, you consent to ChoyFood storing this data in an isolated, encrypted Super Admin vault for fraud-prevention, bogus buyer deterrence, and platform security only. This information is never shown publicly, nor is it accessible to partner restaurants, delivery riders, or non-admin personnel.

2. Separation of Public Display Name and Legal Identity

Customers interact using their chosen public Display Name / Username. The customer's real legal name is encrypted and only retrievable during formal order fraud investigations conducted by verified Super Admins.

3. Choy Wallet Policy

Choy Wallet credit balances are strictly non-withdrawable and intended solely for food and beverage purchases across registered partner stores.
